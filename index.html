Testing

Atomik Labz testing and how to read a COA

Atomik Labz describes its products as third-party tested with accessible certificates of analysis. That is the seller's own statement. The pages read for this guide did not name a testing lab, and this guide has not audited any certificate.

How to check the certificate for your lot

  1. Find the lot number on your vial or packing slip.
  2. Ask for the certificate for that lot. Atomik Labz says COAs are accessible.
  3. Check the lab name and date. A real report names the laboratory and the test date.
  4. Check the compound and amount. A blend should list every component.
  5. Read the purity result. It is usually reported by RP-HPLC as a percent of peak area.
  6. Confirm identity. Mass spectrometry checks that the molecular mass matches.
  7. Contact the seller if anything does not match before you use the product.

Signs of a usable report

A named lab, a test date, a lot number that matches your vial, the compound name and amount, and a purity figure with the method.

Signs to question

No lab name, no lot number, a cropped image, a report for a different compound or a report that cannot be matched to your batch.

Glossary

Terms used on this page

Certificate of analysis (COA)
A lab report for one batch. It shows the lot number, the test method and measured results such as purity.
Lot number
The batch identifier printed on a vial. It must match the lot on the certificate you read.
RP-HPLC
Reversed-phase high-performance liquid chromatography. A method that reports purity as a percent of peak area.
Mass spectrometry
A method that measures molecular mass to check that a peptide has the expected identity.
Lyophilized
Freeze-dried. Peptides are commonly shipped as a powder in a sealed vial.
DAC
Drug affinity complex. A chemical modification that some CJC-1295 products carry and others, labeled W/O DAC, do not.
Blend
A single vial holding more than one compound, such as CJC-1295 with ipamorelin.
Research use only
A seller label meaning the product is for laboratory work, not for people or animals.
